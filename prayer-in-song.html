Prayer in Song

A Theological Approach to Interpreting Hymnody in Its Historical Context

Exploring how Scripture, poetry, music, and history shape the hymns we sing.

The project

Understanding
what we sing.

Prayer in Song is my book and teaching project on the history and theology of hymnody. It brings historical context into conversation with the words of familiar hymns, helping readers and participants approach congregational song as prayer.

The manuscript is a work in progress. The chapters shared here move from the poetry and psalms of the Old Testament into early Christian song.

From research to teaching

Reading closely.
Listening together.

My hymn studies connect a hymn’s words with their scriptural sources, historical setting, and theological meaning.

Teaching materials include manuscript excerpts, study charts, and discussion questions. The available chapters explore literary devices, different kinds of psalms, early Christian canticles, and Psalm 23 alongside “The King of Love My Shepherd Is.”
