Prayer in Song

A Theological Approach to Interpreting Hymnody in Its Historical Context

This book and teaching project explores how the Church's song carries Scripture and theology through history. These manuscript chapters move from Old Testament poetry and the psalms into early Christian song.

The excerpt includes literary-device charts, psalm categories, discussion questions, and a comparison of Psalm 23 with "The King of Love My Shepherd Is."

Holy art Thou, O Father of creation. Holy Thy Son, the author of salvation. Holy Thy Spirit. Praise and adoration now and forever.

Teaching through hymnody.

My hymn-study work brings historical context and theological reading into conversation with familiar texts. The goal is to help people understand what they sing, notice the Scripture behind it, and approach hymnody as a form of prayer.
