About me

A goal in mind.
More to learn.

I'm Marcus Larsen, a technology specialist based in Sheboygan, Wisconsin. I bring a background in computer science and mathematics to practical IT work—and a lifelong learner's curiosity to the questions that come next.

Technology that
helps people work.

At Partners for Community Development, I support IT and telecommunications systems, work with external technology providers, and help plan and maintain infrastructure across multiple sites.

I like projects that ask for both technical thinking and follow-through: getting a new space ready, making a network easier to understand, or turning records into useful information.

My earlier IT experience at Sheboygan Lutheran High School gave me a foundation in support, networking, and infrastructure. I continue to develop those skills alongside graduate study.

I lead hymn studies and write about the history and theology of hymnody through Prayer in Song. I also play timpani with the Sheboygan Pops Concert Band and work with theater lighting at Dare to Dream Theatre.
