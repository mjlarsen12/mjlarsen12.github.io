What can twelve years of fuel receipts tell us about the way we drive?

MAT 320 · Hannah Pratsch & Marcus Larsen · May 2026

670Recorded entries in the source report
2014–2026Observation period

From journal to analysis.

The study follows a 2014 Subaru Forester through changing ownership and driving patterns. Fuel records include date, mileage, gallons, and cost; derived measures include miles per gallon, dollars per gallon, season, and ownership period.

Using R, we explored annual trends and seasonal comparisons with line plots, box plots, one-way ANOVA, and Tukey comparisons.

This is an observational study of one vehicle. Seasonal patterns can be entangled with driving habits, ownership changes, and long-term trends. Repeated measurements are not automatically independent. The original coursework draft is preserved here; its assumption checks and missing-value handling warrant review before treating inferential results as definitive.
